You can show a Privacy Policy and Terms of Service on your Fider site. Once they're set up, Fider links to them from the sign-in and sign-up screens, at `/privacy` and `/terms`.

There are two ways to provide each document:

- [Link to a page you already host](#link-to-existing-pages), for example on your company website.
- [Upload a Markdown file](#upload-markdown-files) that Fider shows itself.

### Link to existing pages

Set these environment variables to the full URLs of your pages:

| Variable | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `LEGAL_PRIVACY_URL` | URL of your Privacy Policy, e.g. `https://example.com/privacy` |
| `LEGAL_TERMS_URL` | URL of your Terms of Service, e.g. `https://example.com/terms` |

Then restart Fider. The links on the sign-in and sign-up screens go to `/privacy` and `/terms`, which redirect to your URLs.

### Upload Markdown files

You need the documents written in Markdown. They **must** be named `privacy.md` and `terms.md`.

Fider reads the files from `/app/etc` inside the container. Put them in a folder on your server and mount it as a volume.

For example, if you followed [Hosting on Docker](/hosting-instance), copy the files to `/var/fider/etc` and add a volume to the `app` service in your `docker-compose.yml`:

```yaml
  app:
    volumes:
      - /var/fider/etc:/app/etc
```

Then restart Fider with `docker compose up -d`.

If you use `docker run`, add `-v /var/fider/etc:/app/etc` instead.

### What users see

When both documents are set up:

- The sign-in page and sign-in dialog show "By signing in, you agree to the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service", with links to both pages.
- When someone creates a new site on the sign-up page, they must tick a box agreeing to both documents before continuing.
- `/privacy` and `/terms` show the documents, or redirect to your URLs. They are visible to everyone, even on a private site.

Things to know:

- **Both documents are required.** Each one can be a URL or a file, and you can mix them. If only one document is set up, Fider doesn't show any links or the agreement box.
- **A URL takes precedence over a file.** If `LEGAL_PRIVACY_URL` is set, Fider ignores `privacy.md`. The same goes for `LEGAL_TERMS_URL` and `terms.md`.
- **Restart after adding or removing the files or variables.** Fider checks whether the files exist once, when it first needs to. Changes to the content of an existing file show up immediately.
- In [multi-tenant](/multi-tenant) mode, the same documents apply to every board.
