You can host your own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service on your Fider site. Once they're set up, Fider shows them at `/privacy` and `/terms`, and links to them from the sign-in and sign-up screens.

### Prerequisites

You need both documents written in Markdown. They **must** be named `privacy.md` and `terms.md`.

### How to

Fider reads both files from `/app/etc` inside the container. Put them in a folder on your server and mount it as a volume.

For example, if you followed [Hosting on Docker](/hosting-instance), copy the files to `/var/fider/etc` and add a volume to the `app` service in your `docker-compose.yml`:

```yaml
  app:
    volumes:
      - /var/fider/etc:/app/etc
```

Then restart Fider with `docker compose up -d`.

If you use `docker run`, add `-v /var/fider/etc:/app/etc` instead.

### What users see

When both files are present:

- The sign-in page and sign-in dialog show "By signing in, you agree to the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service", with links to both pages.
- When someone creates a new site on the sign-up page, they must tick a box agreeing to both documents before continuing.
- `/privacy` and `/terms` show the documents. They are visible to everyone, even on a private site.

Things to know:

- **Both files are required.** If only one of them exists, Fider doesn't show any links or the agreement box.
- **Restart after adding or removing the files.** Fider checks whether the files exist once, when it first needs to. Changes to the content of an existing file show up immediately.
- In [multi-tenant](/multi-tenant) mode, the same documents apply to every board.
